## Overview

This research presents an automated approach for building identification
from satellite imagery.

The proposed methodology consists of two main modules:

1. **HOG-LBP Feature Detection**
2. **Region Refinement**

HOG (Histogram of Oriented Gradients) and LBP (Local Binary Patterns)
are extracted from image regions to identify building and non-building
regions. A CNN classifier is used within the proposed methodology, while
region refinement is applied to improve the identification of building
regions and reduce false detections.

## Research Focus

Building identification from satellite imagery is challenging because
of variations in building size and shape, occlusion by trees and larger
buildings, shadows, weather conditions, and spectral or texture
variations.

The research proposes a two-module approach combining HOG-LBP feature
detection with region refinement for identifying building regions.

## Research Contribution

The research proposes a building identification strategy using linked
HOG and LBP features together with CNN classification and region
refinement.

The paper evaluates the proposed methodology on satellite imagery,
including the Athens dataset, and discusses the effectiveness of the
HOG-LBP detector and region-refinement approach.
